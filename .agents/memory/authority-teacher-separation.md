---
name: Authority and Teacher separation
description: User-mandated exclusive responsibilities for exam management and mark entry.
---

Authority accounts never enter or update student marks. This includes accounts whose role is Teacher but have Authority access, even when using the Teacher panel. Teachers without Authority access enter/update subject marks only under active Authority-created drafts.

Authority manages predefined subjects, component/total rules, exam structures, draft editing/deletion, and publishing. Teachers must not create, delete, or edit those structures or settings.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested exclusive responsibilities and answered “No, Authority accounts never” when asked whether authority-enabled teachers could enter marks from the Teacher panel.

**How to apply:** Treat Authority as an account-level exclusion from mark entry, not merely a selected-panel preference. Do not restore mark-entry access through portal switching, legacy result endpoints, or standalone OMR saves. Preserve Authority's structural editing of active and published drafts and students' published-result viewing.
